[[written august 13, 2026]]

It has come to my attention that you have stumbled on this site. Congratulations!

My name is Indira and I make art under the name voyde. I'm also plenty happy to go by voyde, but it doesn't come naturally so I don't really force it. I'm also pretty happy with my name that my mom gave me. The story goes that as I was being born, Indira Ghandi's death was mentioned on the hospital TV, and my mom (or maybe dad?) was like, yeah that's the name.

I like making art and interactive experiences in unreal engine, but lately I've been having a lot of fun designing things that I think would make a good t-shirt, except I don't believe in mass production so I haven't made them into anything. I think booty shorts are also on the table for these designs. My art practice started when I was young drawing my little original characters. I like drawing beautiful women and weird creatures - not together typically, sometimes the women are creatures though. 

A lot of my art has been describe as hypnagogic psychedelia, surrealist and dreamlike. I spent a lot of my life feeling disembodied and disassociated from reality, escaping in thoughts, or focusing on patterns until they started moving. I don't think this is necessarily a negative thing but I wasn't always practicing escapism for positive reasons. I am a lot happier with my present life, but still find comfort in recreating the little recesses of my mind, and I like sharing the act of looking at a pretty texture and undulation of an object via some fun broken shader with people who might like to look at it too. That looks like [[this-->https://www.youtube.com/@voydescreamsback]] and has been written about in [[KillScreen-->https://www.killscreen.com/indira-ardolics-live-coding-performances-turn-game-engines-into-instruments/]].

I grew up in Staten Island which was relatively normal, I guess. You kind of need a car to get around there. I lived walking distance from the beach and a few parks where I'd look for frogs and deer. (Not like a playground park, it was like a wetlands that the parks department didn't care to manage.) I spent a lot of time walking there with my childhood dog, a Siberian Husky who was black and white with bright blue eyes. She was my favorite!!! We didn't train her for shit though. She didn't really listen other than "sit," sometimes, and was not motivated by treats. She inexplicably got diabetes when she was older and needed insulin shots. It was just one of those things that we didn't really have control over. Dogs' health is just kind of like that when they get older. But anyway. She was crazy soft and super cute. Very sweet and to herself, so you'd have to bother her for affection. I didn't actually find any frogs or deer when I'd walk her in those woods, it was only well after she had passed and I was on my Mandated Walks during pandemic lockdown that I would see deer, frogs, snakes, etc. I think she scared them all away! 

One time I found some organs in those woods, and another time, a fully intact deer skull (no antlers). Hunting isn't allowed in the area, so the animal organs were super weird. I found a parks department person a few hours later and told them about it. They took me on their little open door trucks that they drive around and I led them right to the spot, cuz I knew the trails so well there... Well, it wasn't a large spot, but with the winding trails and my absolute lack of directional awareness, it's impressive, I promise. 

So I am starting this webring type of website because I had been long convinced by [[Mark Stephen Meadows-->https://markmeadows.com/]] and more recently helped into action by a new friend, [[Mochee-->Mochee.xyz]].

For a while, I've been super disillusioned with the current standards of websites, staying in touch, and general expectations for presentation of the self in digital spaces to the point where I've refused to renew my previous domain name and partake in this nonsense. NONSENSE! Well, I *do* still partake in the nonsense via popular social mediums, deleting apps doesn't erase them from being accessible through a web browser. I miss the old internet, uniqueness, individuality, imperfection, the beauty in the hardware limitations, the implication of creating nuanced imagery to mimic real world phenomena instead of simulate it, an illusory web. Maybe this is supposed to be an ongoing love letter.

And back to the hate, I'm a libra so I might balance this out elsewhere, but I'm a scorpio rising so I might keep that to myself. This attention maximizing culture we live in is sucking my soul out of my eye sockets. That's the real reason I have chronic dry eye, not the lasers a bald man pointed into them for 20/20 vision. No. It's my fucking soul evaporating. The seconds, minutes, hours, days of my life that are being stolen - to generate metrics, to generate data, to sell to someone, to posit as useful, to turn a profit. That is my life. I feel sick in my chest and soul when I imagine death, the end, and the fated "life flashing before me" conjuring up ANY amount of time spent scrolling, the type of endless scrolling that has ultimately reduced my grey matter, attention span, lust for life, self confidence, memory retention. God, the list goes on. I am and have been chronically online. (I wouldn't say by choice. Survival looks different for everyone.) I mean, this, is decidedly online. But this (in reference to this site) is meant to recontextualize my relationship with the internet, to reclaim some sense of agency, intimacy, personality that is otherwise stripped when platforms exist to commodity, easily reshare, repurpose, and remove meaning.

So yeah, you know what, as much as my gray matter hath shrunken, I do like the internet. That's kind of the point. When I'm a ghost, I want to be able to still play video games and go online sometimes. That's another thing I'm afraid of. I can't keep playing games? I dunno guys. That's kind of messed up.
